Size & space

Choosing an XL plush for your space

Start with the spot where your plush will sit. Measure the available height and floor or shelf space, then compare those dimensions with the product measurements. A lifestyle photo can help you imagine scale, but listed measurements are the best reference.

For a floor piece, leave room to walk around it and consider how it will look beside nearby furniture. For a bed or chair, check the width as well as the height so your plush feels at home without crowding the space.

Look closer

The little details that make a plush

Notice the overall silhouette first, then look at the details that give a plush its character: the pile and texture of the fabric, the placement of its features, and the way seams and trims are finished.

Product pages should make these details easy to understand with clear photos and straightforward descriptions. If a material, finish, or construction detail matters to you, check the listing for specifics rather than guessing from an image.

Care notes

Simple ways to care for a favorite

Care varies by fabric, filling, and construction, so start with the care instructions supplied with the specific product. When those instructions allow, spot-clean gently with a soft cloth and let the plush air-dry fully.

Avoid machine washing or soaking unless the product’s care label explicitly says it is suitable. For oversized plush, check that you have enough room to clean and dry it safely before bringing it home.
